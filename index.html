Scoring

Each matching die beyond three doubles the value of the three of a kind: four 4s are worth 800, five 1,600 and six 3,200.

  • Roll six dice. After every roll you must set aside at least one scoring die.
  • Combinations only count with dice from the same roll.
  • Then choose: roll the remaining dice again, or bank and add this turn’s points to your score.
  • If a roll scores nothing, it’s a farkle: you lose everything from that turn.
  • If you set aside all six, it’s hot dice: roll all six again and keep adding.
  • The first to reach the goal wins.
Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Warhorse Studios. Scoring as in Kingdom Come: Deliverance II, pure luck: no weighted dice, no badges.
